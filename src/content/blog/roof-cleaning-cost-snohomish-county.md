## What actually drives the price

### Roof area and complexity

Square footage is the starting point, but a cut-up roof with lots of gables, valleys, dormers and
skylights takes far longer than a simple gable roof of the same area. Every edge, every piece of
flashing and every skylight needs to be worked around carefully.

### How much growth is on it

A roof with a light green algae film cleans quickly. A north-facing slope under fir trees with
three-inch moss cushions and established lichen needs more product, a longer dwell time, and often
a follow-up visit a few weeks later once the dead growth has released. That is real labor and
material, and it shows up in the quote.

### Access and safety

Steep pitch, three stories, a roof you can only reach over a landscaped bed or a fragile
sunroom — anything that slows safe access adds time. Single-story homes with clear ground on all
sides are the cheapest to service.

## Soft wash vs. pressure washing — the expensive mistake

This is the part that costs homeowners the most money, and it does not show up on the invoice.

Asphalt shingles are protected by a layer of mineral granules. **High pressure blasts those
granules off**, taking years off the roof's life and voiding the manufacturer's warranty. A
pressure-washed roof can look clean for a season and then fail early — a $9,000 problem to fix a
$149 "deal."

A [soft wash](/services/soft-washing) uses low pressure — barely more than a garden hose — and
professional biocides that kill moss, lichen and Gloeocapsa algae (the black streaking) at the
root. Nothing is blasted. The growth dies, then rain and wind clear it over the following weeks.
It is the method every shingle manufacturer and the [ARMA](https://www.asphaltroofing.org/)
recommends.

If a quote does not say "soft wash" or "no pressure," ask before you book.

## What a fair quote includes

- A clear method: low-pressure soft wash, not pressure washing
- Moss, lichen and black-streak treatment at the root
- A **full rinse of the roof** once the treatment has dwelled
- Protection for gutters, gutter guards, skylights and landscaping
- Gutter debris from the fallen moss flushed out on completion
- A firm price, not a "starting at" that balloons on the day

## How often you will need it

In our climate, most Snohomish County roofs need a soft wash **every 2 to 4 years**. Roofs under
heavy fir shade or on the north side of a hill are closer to every two years. Keeping the
[gutters clear](/services/gutter-cleaning) and the roof clear of debris stretches that interval
out, and keeps each visit cheaper because there is less growth to remove.
