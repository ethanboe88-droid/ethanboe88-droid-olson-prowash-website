## Why exteriors get dirty fast in the Puget Sound area

Our climate is hard on siding, roofs and concrete:

- **Rain and marine humidity** feed green algae, black mildew and moss almost year-round, especially
  on north-facing walls and shaded roofs.
- **Evergreen canopy** drops needles, sap and debris that stain and hold moisture against surfaces.
- **Long, wet winters** keep decks, walkways and patios damp for months, so growth never fully dries out.
- **Mild temperatures** mean moss and lichen keep spreading even in the cold season.

The result: most homes here need attention every year, and any roof with visible moss or black
streaking is overdue.

## The 7 things that separate a premium company from a cheap one

### 1. They soft wash what should be soft washed

High pressure belongs on concrete and brick — not on shingles, siding, LP SmartSide, cedar or
stucco. A quality company uses [soft washing](/services#soft-washing): low pressure plus professional
detergents that kill moss and algae at the root. If a "pressure washer" wants to put 3,000 PSI on
your roof, keep looking.

### 2. They are licensed and insured — and will prove it

In Washington, ask for proof of a contractor registration and a certificate of insurance. Any
legitimate operator can produce both in a few minutes. This protects you if a ladder goes through a
window or a worker is hurt on your property.

### 3. Their quote is itemized and firm

You should see the surfaces, the method and the price in writing before anyone books a date. "We'll
figure it out when we get there" is how a $300 job becomes a $700 invoice.

### 4. They protect your landscaping

Detergents should be applied at safe dilution, beds pre-wet and rinsed, and sensitive plants covered.
A good crew treats your rhododendrons and ferns like they treat your house.

### 5. They show up like professionals

Uniformed crew, clean truck, arrival window honored, a walk-through when they finish. The little
signals tell you how the whole job will go.

### 6. They document results

Before-and-after photos on commercial work as standard, and available on any residential job you
want them on. Nothing to hide.

### 7. They stand behind the work

A real satisfaction guarantee: if a spot gets missed, they come back without an argument.
