## Why exteriors get dirty fast around here

The Puget Sound climate is brutal on siding, roofs and concrete:

- **Rain and marine humidity** feed green algae, black mildew and moss almost year-round,
  especially on north-facing walls and shaded roofs.
- **Evergreen canopy** drops needles, sap and debris that stain surfaces and hold moisture.
- **Mild winters** mean moss and lichen keep spreading even in the cold months — they never fully
  dry out.
- **Freeze-thaw cycles** work grime deep into porous concrete and pavers.

The result: most homes here need attention every year, and any roof with visible moss clumps or
black streaking is already overdue.

## The 7 things that separate a premium company from a cheap one

### 1. They soft wash what should be soft washed

High pressure belongs on concrete and brick — never on shingles, siding, LP SmartSide, cedar or
stucco. A quality company uses [soft washing](/services/soft-washing): low pressure plus
professional biocides that kill moss and algae at the root. If a "pressure washer" wants to put
3,000 PSI on your roof, keep looking.

### 2. They are licensed and insured — and will prove it

In Washington, ask for a contractor registration number and a certificate of insurance. Any
legitimate operator produces both in minutes. This protects you if a ladder goes through a window
or a worker is hurt on your property.

### 3. Their quote is itemized and firm

You should see the surfaces, the method and the price in writing before anyone books a date.
"We'll figure it out when we get there" is how a $300 job becomes a $700 invoice.

### 4. They protect your landscaping

Detergents should be applied at safe dilution, beds pre-wet and rinsed, and sensitive plants
covered. A good crew treats your rhododendrons and ferns like they treat your house.

### 5. They show up like professionals

Uniformed crew, clean truck, arrival window honored, a walk-through when they finish. The small
signals tell you how the whole job will go.

### 6. They document results

Before-and-after photos on commercial work as standard, and available on any residential job you
want them on. Nothing to hide.

### 7. They stand behind the work

A real satisfaction guarantee: if a spot gets missed, they come back without an argument.
