## Common roof problems in Marysville

**Algae streaking on the drier lots.** The more open subdivisions get a lot of black
*Gloeocapsa* algae staining down the slopes — cosmetic at first, but it spreads every wet season
and the stain sets in.

**Heavy moss near the sloughs and tree cover.** Homes near Ebey and Allen Creek, and anywhere with
mature firs and maples, grow thick moss cushions in the valleys and on the north slopes.

**Needle load on north-county lots.** Douglas fir and cedar drop year-round here. That debris traps
moisture on the shingles and feeds regrowth fast.

**Aging shingle roofs.** A lot of Marysville's housing stock is on its first shingle layer nearing
20+ years — the age where moss does the most harm to the shingle life that's left.

## Our roof cleaning process

1. **Inspect** the roof and gutters before quoting — pitch, shingle condition, moss severity.
2. **Protect** landscaping — pre-wet and cover.
3. **Treat** with our in-house roof solution at low pressure; let it **dwell** to kill moss,
   algae and lichen at the root.
4. **Rinse** fully and gently.
5. **Clear** loosened debris off the roof and out of the gutters.

It's a [soft wash](/services/soft-washing), never a pressure wash — done to
<a href="https://www.asphaltroofing.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ARMA</a> and shingle-manufacturer guidelines so your warranty
stays intact. Full details on our [roof cleaning service page](/services/roof-cleaning), and the
method explained in
[soft wash vs pressure wash for your roof](/blog/soft-wash-vs-pressure-wash-roof).
