If you live in Lake Stevens, you already know what a wet winter does to a north-facing roof.
By February there's a green fuzz along the shingle edges, black streaking down the slopes, and
maybe a clump of moss growing in a valley where the fir needles pile up. The first question
everyone asks me is the same: **what's this going to cost to fix?**

Here are honest 2026 numbers for our area, what moves the price up or down, and how to tell a
fair quote from one that's going to cost you a roof.

## The short answer

For a typical single-family home in Lake Stevens, a professional [roof soft wash](/services/roof-cleaning)
runs **$299 to about $950**, with most homes landing in the **$450–$750** range. Larger footprints,
steep pitches, and heavy moss push it higher. Small ramblers with light streaking come in at the
bottom of the range.

These are soft wash prices — low pressure, a cleaning treatment that's left to dwell, then a full
rinse. They are **not** the price of someone running a pressure washer across your shingles, which
is cheaper up front and far more expensive later (more on that below).

## What actually drives the price

**Roof size and layout.** More square footage is more product and more time. A simple gable roof
is quick; a roof chopped up with dormers, valleys, skylights and multiple levels takes longer to
cover safely.

**Pitch and access.** A walkable 4:12 pitch is straightforward. A steep 9:12 or a three-story wall
of roof means roped access, more setup, and more safety gear — that's real labor and it shows in
the quote.

**Moss severity.** Light black streaking (that's <a href="https://www.asphaltroofing.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">gloeocapsa</a> algae,
not dirt) treats and rinses easily. Thick moss mats with root systems in the shingle seams need a
gentler, more patient approach and sometimes a follow-up visit as the dead moss releases over the
following weeks. Heavier growth costs more.

**Surroundings.** Big-leaf maples and Douglas firs directly over the roof mean more debris, more
shade, and faster regrowth — some companies price that in.

**Add-ons.** Most Lake Stevens homeowners bundle a [gutter cleaning](/services/gutter-cleaning) and
a [house wash](/services/house-washing) with the roof while we're already set up on site, which is
cheaper per service than booking them separately. If you want the black "tiger stripe" staining
taken off the outside of the gutters, that's our separate
[gutter brightening](/services/gutter-brightening) service.

## How to spot a risky quote

- **A price far below $299 for a whole roof.** Nobody can cover a house roof with treatment,
  protect the landscaping, and rinse it properly for $150. Something is getting skipped — usually
  the dwell time or the plant protection.
- **The word "pressure" anywhere near your shingles.** High pressure blasts the protective granules
  off asphalt shingle and voids most manufacturer warranties. It looks clean for a season, then the
  roof ages years in a hurry.
- **No proof of license or insurance.** In Washington you can
  <a href="https://www.lni.wa.gov/licensing-permits/contractors/verify-a-contractor-tradesperson-or-business/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">verify any contractor</a>
  through L&I in about thirty seconds. We're licensed and insured — registration #OLSONLP747MF.
- **No before/after work and no reviews.** Ours are on our [reviews page](/reviews) and in our
  [project gallery](/gallery).

## Frequently asked questions

**How long does a roof cleaning take?**
Most Lake Stevens homes are a single visit of two to four hours. Heavy moss jobs sometimes need a
short return trip once the dead growth has fully released.

**Will the moss come back?**
Eventually — this is the Pacific Northwest. A good soft wash keeps a roof clear for roughly two to
three years. Keeping the roof and gutters free of debris and trimming back overhanging branches
stretches that out.

**Do you clean the roof in winter?**
We can, but late spring and early fall give the treatment the mild, dry-ish conditions it likes.
We don't treat in a hard freeze.

**Is soft washing safe for my plants?**
Yes, when it's done right. We pre-wet and rinse landscaping before, during and after, and we keep
runoff controlled. Washington's <a href="https://ecology.wa.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Ecology</a> has good guidance
on keeping wash water out of storm drains, and we follow it.

**Can you do the gutters and house at the same time?**
Yes, and it's cheaper that way. A roof-plus-gutters-plus-house-wash visit is the most common
booking we run in Lake Stevens.
