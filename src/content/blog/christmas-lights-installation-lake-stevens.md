Lake Stevens does the holidays right — the lake, the tree lighting, whole streets lit up. If you
want your house to be one of the sharp ones without spending a cold weekend on a wet roof, here's
how our [Christmas light installation](/services/christmas-light-installation) works, what it
costs, and why the calendar fills up fast.

## What's included

- **Design consultation.** We walk your rooflines, peaks, gables and window runs and lay out the
  look with you — warm white or multicolor, C9, C7 or mini bulbs, plus wreaths, garland and tree
  wraps.
- **Custom-cut commercial-grade lights.** Every strand is cut and end-capped to the exact length
  of that section of your house — no bunching at the corners, no dark gaps. Commercial C9/C7/mini
  bulbs on weather-rated wire, not big-box strings.
- **Professional install on your date.** Clips sized to your shingles or gutters, wiring routed
  out of sight, everything tied into a timer and tested before we leave.
- **In-season service.** A bulb or section fails, we come swap it — no ladder for you.
- **January takedown.**
- **Labeled storage.** Your custom set is stored so next year's re-hang is fast and cheaper.

You own the lights after the first season. Full detail on the
[service page](/services/christmas-light-installation), and the case for custom-cut over
store-bought in

## Pricing

**From $399 for the first season**, which covers the measure, the custom-cut commercial-grade
lights, and the install. Most Lake Stevens homes run **$399–$1,200** depending on roofline length
and design — a single-story ranch with a clean front is at the low end; a two-story with multiple
gables, window outlines and landscape lighting is higher.

**Re-hang seasons cost less.** Because you own the set and it's pre-cut to your house, following
years are mostly labor — install, in-season service, takedown and storage — at a lower flat rate.

## Why book early

Installs run **early November through mid-December**, and the good slots go first — people book
their install date in September and October. Booking early means:

- You pick your install week instead of taking what's left
- Design and custom-cutting are done ahead, so install day is quick
- You're lit up right after Thanksgiving when the neighborhood turns on

Waiting until December usually means a waitlist.

## Custom vs. standard designs

A **standard** design outlines your main roofline and maybe the garage — clean, classic, the most
popular choice. A **custom** design adds window outlines, multiple gables and peaks, porch
columns, wreaths, garland along railings, and wrapped trees or shrubs. We'll quote both so you can
see the difference.

## Safety — the real reason to hire this out

Steep, wet, moss-slick roofs in November and December are the worst place to be on a ladder. The
<a href="https://www.esfi.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Electrical Safety Foundation International</a> and the
<a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission</a> both publish yearly numbers on
holiday-decorating falls and electrical fires — most from overloaded circuits, damaged strings, and
ladder work in bad footing. Commercial-grade wire on a proper timer, installed from stable
setups, takes that risk off your plate.
