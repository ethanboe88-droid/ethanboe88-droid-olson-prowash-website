## Why exteriors get dirty fast in the Northwoods

Our climate is hard on siding, roofs and concrete:

- **Tree cover and humidity** feed green algae, black mildew and gray lichen, especially on north-
  facing walls and shaded roofs.
- **Long winters** trap moisture against surfaces under snow and ice for months.
- **Pollen and lake spray** leave a film that dulls color and holds dirt.
- **Freeze-thaw cycles** work grime deep into porous concrete and pavers.

The result: most homes here need attention every one to two years, and roofs with visible streaking
are overdue.

## The 7 things that separate a premium company from a cheap one

### 1. They soft wash what should be soft washed

High pressure belongs on concrete and brick — not on shingles, vinyl, stucco or cedar. A quality
company uses [soft washing](/services#soft-washing): low pressure plus professional detergents that
kill organic growth at the root. If a "pressure washer" wants to put 3,000 PSI on your roof, keep
looking.

### 2. They are licensed and insured — and will prove it

Ask for a certificate of insurance. Any legitimate operator can produce one in a few minutes. This
protects you if a ladder goes through a window or a worker is hurt on your property.

### 3. Their quote is itemized and firm

You should see the surfaces, the method and the price in writing before anyone books a date. "We'll
figure it out when we get there" is how a $300 job becomes a $700 invoice.

### 4. They protect your landscaping

Detergents should be applied at safe dilution, beds should be pre-wet and rinsed, and sensitive
plants covered. A good crew treats your hostas like they treat your house.

### 5. They show up like professionals

Uniformed crew, clean truck, arrival window honored, a walk-through when they finish. The little
signals tell you how the whole job will go.

### 6. They document results

Before-and-after photos on commercial work as standard, and available on any residential job you
want them on. Nothing to hide.

### 7. They stand behind the work

A real satisfaction guarantee: if a spot gets missed, they come back without an argument.

## Questions to ask before you book

1. Will my siding and roof be soft washed or pressure washed?
2. Can you send a certificate of insurance?
3. Is the quote itemized and firm?
4. How do you protect landscaping and light fixtures?
5. What is your guarantee if something is missed?
