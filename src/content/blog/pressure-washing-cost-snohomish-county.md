"Pressure washing" covers a lot of ground — a driveway, a whole house, a deck, a fence, a patio —
and each one prices differently. So a flat "what do you charge?" is hard to answer without knowing
what you're looking at. Here's how [pressure washing](/services/pressure-washing) actually prices
out around Snohomish County in 2026, surface by surface.

## Price ranges by surface

| Surface | Typical 2026 price |
| --- | --- |
| Driveway (2-car) | $99 – $250 |
| Large or oil-stained driveway | $250 – $450 |
| Sidewalks / walkways (added to another job) | $49 – $150 |
| Whole-home wash, single-story (soft wash) | $199 – $400 |
| Whole-home wash, two-story (soft wash) | $400 – $700 |
| Deck or patio | $99 – $400 |
| Fence (by length and number of sides) | $99 – $300 |
| Paver patio or walkway (sand re-set extra) | $150 – $450 |

Concrete flatwork — driveways, walks, patios — is where "pressure washing" is literally the right
tool: controlled high pressure through a surface cleaner for an even, streak-free finish. Siding,
roofs and painted or stained surfaces are **not** pressure washed; those get a low-pressure
[soft wash](/services/soft-washing) instead, which is why a "house pressure washing" quote should
really be a soft wash quote.

## What affects the price

**Square footage and layout.** Bigger area, more time. A wide-open driveway cleans fast; steps,
borders, tight side yards and detail work around a porch add labor.

**How bad it is.** A driveway cleaned every year just needs a pass. One that hasn't been touched in
a decade — with set-in moss, tire marks, leaf tannin and oil — takes pre-treatment, dwell time and
sometimes a second pass.

**Oil and rust.** Engine oil, transmission fluid and rust stains need specific treatments and
still may not lift 100%. We're honest about that before we start. Hot water is available on the
truck for grease, gum and oil when a job calls for it.

**Access to water.** Almost every home is fine. Rural properties on a low-output well sometimes
need us to bring a buffer tank, which is a small add.

**Our concrete post-treatment.** On flatwork we finish with a treatment that kills the organic
growth in the pores, brightens the concrete back toward like-new, and helps it stay clean longer.
It's part of the service, not an upsell.

## DIY vs. hiring a pro

| | DIY | Professional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up-front cost | $120–$400 for a decent electric or gas washer | $99+ per job |
| Driveway result | Streaky "zebra stripes" are common without a surface cleaner | Even, edge-to-edge finish |
| Time | Half a day for a driveway, first time out | 45–90 minutes |
| Risk | Etched concrete, cracked siding, water in the crawlspace, ladder falls | Carried by the contractor's insurance |
| Stain treatment | Whatever's at the hardware store | Commercial pre-treatments and hot water |

A rented or bought washer makes sense if you enjoy the work and stick to concrete. For siding,
anything up high, or heavy staining, the math usually favors a pro once you count your time and the
cost of a mistake. The U.S.
<a href="https://www.cpsc.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Consumer Product Safety Commission</a> logs thousands of pressure-washer
injuries a year — the spray will cut skin and drive bacteria into a wound.

## Runoff and doing it right

Wash water carries detergent, dirt and hydrocarbons, and in most of Snohomish County the storm
drains run straight to a creek or to Puget Sound. We follow
<a href="https://ecology.wa.gov/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Washington Department of Ecology</a> guidance on containing and filtering
runoff — worth knowing whether you hire out or do it yourself.

## Frequently asked questions

**How often should I pressure wash my driveway?**
Once a year keeps it bright in our climate. Go longer and the moss and tannin set in, which turns a
$99–$150 maintenance clean into a $250+ restoration.

**Can you get oil stains out?**
We can usually lighten them a lot and sometimes remove them fully, depending on how old and how
deep. We'll tell you honestly what to expect before we quote.

**Do you move my cars and patio furniture?**
We'll ask you to move vehicles and anything fragile. We'll shift lightweight furniture and put it
back.

**Is a house wash the same as pressure washing?**
No. A house wash is a low-pressure soft wash with detergent — safe for siding, paint and trim.
See our [soft wash vs pressure wash guide](/blog/soft-wash-vs-pressure-wash-washington-home).

**Do you bundle services?**
Yes. Driveway + house wash + gutters in one visit is cheaper per service than three separate trips,
and it's our most common booking.
