## The problem

Long rooflines on acreage mean long gutter runs, and the run along the driveway side had gone
past "needs cleaning" into "growing things." A solid rope of maple leaves and grit ran the full
length, water was overflowing at the low end, and the downspout was blocked.

On a rural lot, an overflowing gutter isn't just cosmetic — it dumps water against the foundation
and the crawlspace vents.

## What we did

- **Hand-cleared the whole run.** Debris was scooped by hand and hauled away — not blown onto the
  gravel or the beds.
- **Cleared the downspout.** The blockage at the elbow was broken loose and the downspout flushed
  until it ran clean.
- **Flow-tested every run.** Water was run across all the gutters on that elevation to confirm
  full flow to the ground.
- **Faces soft washed.** The "tiger stripe" staining on the exterior faces was washed off so the
  line looked new.

## The result

The run drains properly again and the overflow at the foundation stopped. With this many trees
and this much linear footage, the customer added a
[Raptor gutter guard](/services/gutter-guard-installation) section on the worst elevation so it
doesn't pack in again next fall.
